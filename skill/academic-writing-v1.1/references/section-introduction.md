# 引言（Introduction）逐句写作引导

来源：Glasman-Deal, *Science Research Writing for Native and Non-Native Speakers of English*, 2nd ed.（World Scientific, 2021），Unit 1（pp. 3–17 建立模型；pp. 45–52 语言与句式）。

## 句子级结构（按写作顺序）

引言由"宽 → 窄"的信息流组成：从领域重要性/背景事实出发，逐步收窄到本文要解决的空白。下面 7 个"句子功能位"即书中 Generic Introduction Model 的句子级展开，按写作顺序逐个写：

### 句子位 1：确立主题/领域的重要性
- 作用：告诉读者这个话题为何值得关注（通常出现在第一句）。若领域极窄、读者都是圈内人，可改为从"研究焦点/定义/必要事实"开始。
- 句式模板：
  - `___ has received much attention in recent years due to its ___ properties.`
  - `Much study in recent years has focused on ___.`
  - `___ plays a key/major role in ___.`
  - `Since the discovery of ___, many ___ approaches have been developed.`
- 例句（书中原句，均可作首句）：
  - `Biomass-derived polylactide (PLA) has received much attention in recent years due to its biodegradable and biocompatible properties.`
  - `The status and function of the port has evolved rapidly in recent years.`
  - `Since the discovery of the first isolated graphene layer, many chemical approaches have been developed.`
  - `Fourier transforms are widely used in image processing to characterise textures in images.`
  - `Heat transfer phenomena play an important role in welding.`
  - `The increasing pressure to eliminate lead has stimulated great interest in the search for lead-free solders.`
  - `Control of serum phosphorus levels is a central goal in the management of patients with chronic renal failure.`
- 时态提示：recent/in recent years 等时间词通常配现在完成时（has focused on / has evolved）；聚焦当前状况则用一般现在时（plays a role / is a central goal）。可在时间词后加明确年份（如 since 2018）以延长论文时效。
- 写作检查：我的目标期刊里，同类论文第一句是否也这样确立重要性？我是否用了 recent/in recent years 这类时间词并配了正确时态？

### 句子位 2：提供背景事实信息
- 作用：用公认事实给读者（尤其是跨学科读者）一个共同起点；一般现在时表"公认事实"。
- 句式模板：
  - `___ is/are produced from ___.`
  - `___ is a ___ found/occurring in ___.`
  - `___ is a multifactorial ___, characterized by ___.`
- 例句：`Biomass-derived PLA is produced from renewable feedstocks such as corn and sugar cane.`（书中原句）
- 写作检查：这个事实是"公认事实"吗？它的宽窄是否匹配期刊读者群（读者越宽，起点越要一般，必要时可先给"meeting-place"级的最一般事实）？该事实是否需要引文（已公认的事实可不再引）？

### 句子位 3：用引文支撑、细化背景
- 作用：在背景基础上给出更具体的信息，并用引用文献支撑事实与"重要性"论断。
- 句式模板：
  - `It has ___ properties for use in ___ such as ___ [refs].`
  - `___ has been shown to ___ [refs].`
  - `___ is widely used in ___ to ___ [refs].`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `It has outstanding molding properties for use in biomedical applications such as scaffolds for tissue engineering.`
  - `NDE systems have been applied to the inspection of pipelines.`
  - `Several authors have developed compensation techniques for temperature changes.`
  - `The effect of keratin on the mechanical properties of copolymer systems was demonstrated more than two years ago.`
- 写作检查：我加的引文是否恰好覆盖这句话中来自该研究的部分（引用位置要明确对应内容，避免让读者误以为整句都出自该文献）？

### 句子位 4：描述一般问题领域/当前研究焦点
- 作用：指出该领域当前共同关注的问题（注意：还不是你自己要解决的"特定问题"）。
- 句式模板：
  - `However, ___ has significantly limited ___.`
  - `A key challenge/current focus in this field is ___.`
  - `___ require/s ___, so although ___ has/have been used, these are not appropriate for ___.`
- 例句：
  - `However, its weakness under impact has significantly limited its use.`（书中原句）
  - `Biomedical applications require materials with the lowest possible toxicity so although metals such as aluminium have been used to strengthen PLA for industrial applications, these are not appropriate for biomedical use.`（书中原句）
- 写作检查：我描述的是"领域层面的问题"，还是过早跳进了自己研究的具体问题？

### 句子位 5：把问题领域连接到已发表研究（研究地图）
- 作用：简述前人/当前研究及其贡献，把读者带到你的研究动机附近。组织顺序可用"由一般到具体 / 按方法·理论分组 / 按时间"；用连接词把文献串成一条叙事线，避免"购物清单"或"打网球式"的来回转折。
- 句式模板：
  - `One way to ___ is to ___, and there has been extensive research regarding ___ [refs].`
  - `For example, [ref] showed that ___ could be achieved using ___.`
  - 连接句式（书中原列）：`[ref] also observed that…` / `[ref] resolved this by…` / `[ref] were the first to…` / `A pioneering study by [ref] demonstrated…` / `A similar approach was used by [ref], who…` / `An alternative approach was proposed by [ref], who…` / `It was later shown by [ref] that…` / `Taken together, these studies suggest that…`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `One way to strengthen polymers for biomedical applications is to incorporate a layer of keratin, a material that occurs naturally in the mammalian anatomy, and there has been extensive research regarding keratin-PLA composites.`
  - `For example, Penney et al. (2012) showed that incorporation of keratin fillers in the PLA matrix could be achieved using blending techniques.`
  - `The advantages and limitations of each of these techniques have been evaluated by different authors.`
- 写作检查：我选的每篇文献是否都"相关且必要"，并且顺着一条线索导向我的动机？句与句之间是否用连接词（also / In contrast / Subsequently / Taken together）显式推进，而非仅罗列引用？

### 句子位 6：指出研究空白/问题
- 作用：明确前人研究的缺口或不足，从而引出本文；通常用 However/Although 等信号词提示读者（这些词有"即时识别"作用）。专业写作中空白通常以陈述/预测/假设写出，而非问句。
- 句式模板：
  - `However, although ___ was demonstrated, ___, and little attention has been paid to ___.`
  - `However, ___ remains unclear/controversial.`
  - `Few studies have focused on ___.` / `Little is known about ___.`
  - `To the best of our knowledge, ___ has not been addressed.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `However, although the effect of keratin on the mechanical properties of copolymer systems was demonstrated over two years ago, keratin adds considerably to the weight of the composite, and little attention has been paid to the selection of an alternative biocompatible material.`
  - `Little attention has been paid to the selection of an alternative biocompatible material.`
  - `How propofol acts on neural circuits to produce unconsciousness remains unclear.`
  - `Although there is general agreement regarding the timing of sea-level events, their amplitude remains controversial.`
  - `The main drawback of this approach is that it is unable to provide quantitative information.`
  - `Little is known about these early differentiating cells...`
  - `Determining the function of these proteins remains a challenge.`
- 写作检查：空白是否以陈述而非问句写出？语言是否礼貌、客观（如 little attention has been paid / remains unclear），既不冒犯前人，也不过度谦虚？

### 句子位 7：描述本文/研究
- 作用：收窄到本文本身——可描述它、说清目的/焦点、给出结构，或简述方法、主要结果（常配"happy words"明确价值）。
- 句式模板：
  - `The present paper presents ___.`
  - `In this paper we present a ___ method for ___.`
  - `Our study focuses on ___.` / `To address this question, we investigated ___.`
  - `The main objective of this study was to describe and examine ___.`
  - `This paper is organised as follows: ...`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `The present paper presents a set of criteria for selecting such a component.`
  - `In this paper we present a robust method for...`
  - `In this study, we carefully identified and isolated...`
  - `New correlations were developed with excellent results.`
  - `To our knowledge, this is the first demonstration of the...`
  - `We show that use of glass reactors can be effective.`
  - `Section V provides several relevant conclusions obtained from the study reported in this paper.`
  - `Our design is both simple and accurate, and can be fully integrated into...`
- 时态提示：描述本文用现在时（This paper is…/This study focuses on…）；谈"目的"可用过去时（The aim was…）或现在时（The aim is…，尤其目的只部分达成时）。
- 写作检查：描述本文是否用对时态？是否用 "happy words"（novel, robust, successful, to our knowledge the first）明确写出价值？

### 常用短语速查（Unit 1, pp. 45–52）

- 确立重要性：`(an) important/major/crucial/key issue`、`much study in recent years`、`widespread`、`plays a key/major role`、`of growing/great interest`、`an increasing number`、`a dramatic increase`、`worldwide`。
- 研究动词（描述前人/本研究活动）：`investigate, demonstrate, develop, propose, show, suggest, find, report, measure, evaluate, compare, examine, establish, confirm, reveal, identify, extend, prove`。
- 空白（问题/批评）：`limited, unclear, insufficient, poor, time-consuming, inaccurate, a gap, a limitation, a drawback, little is known, few studies have…, remains controversial, not addressed, more work is needed, to the best of our knowledge`。
- 空白（研究机会）：`ideal candidate`、`there is an urgent need`、`it is/seems possible that…`（常配 may/might/could/would）。
- "happy" 词（描述本文价值）：`novel, new, robust, simple, accurate, powerful, promising, successful, effective, efficient, superior, valuable, straightforward`。

## 语言与写作技巧要点（Unit 1.5, pp. 53–68）

### 动词时态：Past Simple vs Present Simple vs Present Perfect
- `They found that the pressure increased as the temperature rose.`（过去时：仅描述该研究发现，与那项研究挂钩，不表永久真理）
- `They found that the pressure increases as the temperature rises.`（现在时：相信发现足够可靠、构成永久真理）
- 用 Past Simple 还是 Present Perfect 看"与现在的相关性"：`...little attention has been paid to...`（现在完成时）表示这是"当前仍存在的空白"，暗示本文将填补它；若写 `...little attention was paid to...`（过去时）则表示"两年前没被关注"，问题可能已被解决。
- 时态的每一次切换都有意义，都标志着信息功能的转变——不要随意选择或切换时态。写作前先查目标期刊近期文献对同一事实当前用何种时态。

### 被动 / 主动选择（描述本文时）
- 三种写法：①用 we/our；②用被动；③用非人称主语（`This study demonstrates that…` / `Section 1 presents…`）。
- 用 we/our 时要保持指代一致，避免一句指"本文作者"（we investigated）、一句指"整个领域的人"（our knowledge）；若指"人们/普遍认知"，改用 `It is known/thought that…` 更清晰。

### 分段
- 每段应有单一功能，段内每句都为该功能服务，"游离"或无关的句子会打断叙述。
- 研究论文段落平均约 150–170 词；避免大量超长（>230 词）或过短（<80 词）段落。

## 附：摘要（Abstract）模板

来源：同书 Unit 6（pp. 286–289, Generic Abstract Model）。摘要是全文的浓缩，常由 3 组句子功能位组成，且"happy words"（价值词）出现的点位较多：

### 摘要位 1：主题重要性 / 必要背景 + 挑战/问题 + 本文做什么
- 句式模板：`___ is a central/emerging challenge in ___. Here we present a ___ method/approach for ___.`（可合并"本文做什么 + 成就"：`In this paper, we present a novel ___ method that combines ___ to achieve ___.`）
- 例句：`In this paper, we present a novel estimation method that combines the strengths of quality-guided tracking, multi-level correlation, and phase-zero search to achieve high levels of accuracy and robustness.`（书中原句）
- 写作检查：摘要首句能否让只看摘要的读者立刻明白本文在做什么、价值何在？

### 摘要位 2：方法/材料 + 结果 + 含义
- 句式模板：`___ was investigated using ___. ... resulted in ___ / demonstrated ___, suggesting that ___.`（方法与结果可合并：`Specifically, addition of ___ resulted in significant inhibition.`）
- 例句：`Specifically, addition of ≥ 13.3 mg/ml of 45S5 bioactive glass or Sr50, or ≥ 6.7 mg/ml of Sr100, resulted in significant inhibition.`；`Here we demonstrate a method to promote the detachment of microparticles from bubbles using ultrasound…`（书中原句）
- 写作检查：我只挑了"关键"方法与"关键"结果（常带数字），还是把无关细节也塞进了摘要？数字是否加了评价词（only 38% / as high as 38%）防止被误读？

### 摘要位 3：映射到已有知识 + 成就/价值/贡献 + 应用
- 句式模板：`The present model further improves upon the accuracy of existing models by ___, which enables ___.`（或 `These results suggest that ___, providing ___ for ___.`）
- 例句：`...the present model further improves upon the accuracy of existing models by incorporating a more complete version of the Nernst equation, which enables accurate prediction of the cell potential without the use of a fitting voltage.`（书中原句，简化）
- 写作检查：摘要里的含义/结论是否与正文数据一致、没有过度承诺？（不确定时用 may/might/could 或 possible/potential 缓和）
