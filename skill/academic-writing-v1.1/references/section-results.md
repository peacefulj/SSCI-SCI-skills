# 结果（Results）逐句写作引导

来源：Glasman-Deal, *Science Research Writing for Native and Non-Native Speakers of English*, 2nd ed.（World Scientific, 2021），Unit 3（pp. 142–156 建立模型；pp. 169–182 语言与句式）。

## 句子级结构（按写作顺序）

结果部分不是"按时间顺序记录数据"，而是用叙述引导读者走向你的解释与结论（"results do not speak for themselves"）。下面 7 个"句子功能位"即书中 Generic Results Model 的句子级展开：

### 句子位 1：重访文献/目标/假设/方法，或对结果作总体陈述
- 作用：给读者一个平滑过渡——回顾上文的关键点（尤其对直接从标题/摘要跳到结果的"信息浏览型"读者），或先给结果的总体趋势（show the wall before the bricks）。
- 句式模板：
  - `Data obtained in previous ___ studies [refs] found ___. In our study, ___ was/were measured using ___.`
  - 总起：`In general / Overall / In most cases, ___.`
  - 重述目标：`To determine whether ___, we ___.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `Data obtained in previous cycling safety studies used fixed monitors... and found no significant difference in BC and NO2 emissions between cycle lanes... In our study, BC and NO2 concentrations were measured using a PEMS-43...`
  - `In our study, BC and NO2 concentrations were measured using a PEMS-43, a portable emissions measurement system developed by Noxious et al. The PEMS-43 was fixed to a bicycle and recorded the emissions hourly along each route type.`
- 写作检查：读者若没读引言/方法，能否凭这一句就理解我的结果在回答什么问题、用什么方法得到？

### 句子位 2：邀请读者查看图表并说明图表内容
- 作用：在叙述的自然点引导读者看图/表，并提前给出解读框架（若数据可被多解，先评论再邀请看图，避免读者得出不同解读）。
- 句式模板：
  - `Figure 1 shows ___.` / `Figure 1 illustrates ___.`
  - `As can be seen from/in Fig. 1, ___.` / `___ is/are shown in Fig. 1.`
  - `The data in Fig. 1 reveal that ___.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `Figure 1 shows the BC and NO2 concentrations measured hourly on each route type from 6.00 a.m. to 10.00 a.m.`
  - `As is apparent from Fig. 1, the extractibility... did not change significantly.`
  - `The data in Fig. 18 reveal that H3K36 can repair damaged DNA.`
  - `The lack of correlation between IR and PLFA patterns can be seen by comparing Figs 1 and 5.`
  - `Results for individual groups are displayed in Supplementary Figure 5.`
  - `An analysis of the follow-up status of the subjects is presented in Table 2.`
- 写作检查：图表是否放在读者"正想看它"的位置（而非随机散布）？图表若可被多解，我是否先用文字框定了正确解读？是否区分了 from Fig.（从图可推出）与 in Fig.（图中可见）？

### 句子位 3：呈现具体/关键结果（客观 + 主观评价语言）
- 作用：挑出关键结果详细呈现——客观描述之外，加评价语言把读者引向你的理解（如 "as many as 23%" vs "only 23%"）。"你不能用红色打印或感叹号"，必须靠选词传达轻重。
- 句式模板：
  - `We recorded a striking ___ in ___ (Fig. 2).`
  - 客观动词：`increased/decreased/peaked/remained constant/rose/fell/varied`；`It was observed that ___ exhibited higher ___ than ___.`
  - 评价：`striking/significantly/much higher/only slightly/remarkably/virtually unchanged`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `We recorded a striking reduction in the NO2 concentrations along bike paths separated from vehicle traffic, beginning shortly after 10.00 a.m. (Figure 2).`
  - `Protein expression peaked at day 3 after infection.`
  - `The frequencies of the jets were constant, and did not change linearly with velocity.`
  - `It was observed that the female rats exhibited higher levels of plasma corticosterone than male rats.`
  - `Significantly, we found that the activation energy for oxidation in both gases is much higher.`
  - `Overall, there was a close similarity between the biological activity and immunoreactivity of plasma ACTH in the three specimens.`
  - `Survival rates in the steroid treated patients were essentially identical to those of the placebo treated patients.`
  - `The calculated Young's modulus was almost constant in the area above the nucleus.`
  - `Minor clinical abnormalities of nerve function were fairly common in the rheumatoid group.`
  - `In several cases the chromaticity of exposed skin was noticeably different from that of unexposed skin.`
  - `Breaks in nanotubes were rarely observed following specimen cutting.`
  - `Extracts prepared at 0.3 M NaCl were only slightly active while extracts prepared at concentrations of 0.35 0.5 M NaCl were quite active.`
  - `The impacts of other micronutrients were in general quite weak.`
  - `The solenoid valve frequently generated a more accurate pressure response.`
  - `Ageing of the alloy showed that the YS was virtually unchanged.`
  - `The colours of the two lights were found to be extremely similar, if not identical.`
  - `The magnitudes of the ionic displacements were found to be extremely large.`
  - `Notably, the non-magmatic IAB and IIICD irons display overlapping Zn contents, in accord with the previous conclusion that these two groups are closely related.`
  - `The yeast samples were, however, found to be relatively poor sources for dehydrogenases.`
- 写作检查：我是否只用数字（客观）而没有加评价词，导致读者可能误读结果的强弱？是否只详写了"关键"结果、让次要结果退后？评价词是否匹配我想让读者形成的印象？

### 句子位 4：与其他研究/模型/预测结果比较
- 作用：把结果放上"研究地图"，说明它如何推进或修正已有认识；既可对比文献结果，也可对比自己的模型/仿真/预测。
- 句式模板：
  - `In line with results from previous studies, ___ was/were ___. However, ___ was/were significantly different.`
  - `Our results are consistent with ___ [refs].` / `___ compares well with ___.` / `These data appear to confirm ___.`
  - `Contrary to ___, ___.` / `Our results differ from those in the literature.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `In line with results from previous studies, the BC and NO2 concentrations were broadly similar for both route types over this 4-hour period. However, the data obtained by PEMS-43 for journeys later in the day were significantly different.`
  - `The bubble growth predicted from numerical analysis compares well with that obtained in this work.`
  - `These new results are consistent with, and substantially more sensitive than, previously published anisotropy measurements.`
  - `The accumulation rates we observed are in line with those reported from sulfate incorporation methods.`
  - `Thus, the new data from the Human Genome Project tend to corroborate the result in Fig. 2.`
  - `Our results differ from those in the literature, and agree better with published CDF data.`
  - `These data appear to confirm the prevalence of Vitamin D deficiency in the normal population.`
  - `The location of the gas concentration in the simulation is in remarkably good agreement with that of the observed dust lanes.`
- 写作检查：我是否同时比较了"与文献结果"和"与自己的模型/预测结果"？相似/差异程度词（broadly similar, significantly different, in good agreement with, contrary to）是否准确？

### 句子位 5：解释结果（借助已知事实或方法细节）
- 作用：用已知事实或方法细节直接解释"结果为何如此"（含义的展开留给讨论部分）。区分"解释"（为何发生）与"含义"（意味着什么）。
- 句式模板：
  - `___ occurred because material X is able to ___.`
  - `This occurred because we used ___.`
  - `This may be explained by ___.` / `This is in part due to ___.`
- 例句：`V2D RNA is known to persist in the bodies of survivors long after disease onset.`（书中原句，作背景解释用途）
- 补充（书中说明）：解释"如何得到"该结果可用方法细节；解释"为何发生"可用材料性质等已知事实。措辞可用 `This may be explained by ___.` / `This is in part due to ___.`
- 写作检查：我区分了"解释结果"（为何发生）与"结果含义"（意味着什么）吗？解释是否克制，把含义留给了讨论？

### 句子位 6：指出结果中的问题/局限
- 作用：主动承认结果的不完整或不拟合——弱化其影响、给出理由、或给出解决/未来方向。忽略问题会显得你不了解自己的研究。
- 句式模板：
  - `___ is known to slightly alter ___, and the effect of this along ___ was not investigated.`
  - 弱化：`slightly/negligible/not significant/of no consequence/minor`
  - 给方向：`Future work should ___.` / `A further study will ___.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `The presence of vegetation is known to slightly alter BC and NO2 concentrations at different times of day, and the effect of this along the two routes was not investigated.`
  - `Unfortunately, it was not possible to quantify the extent of Zn loss from the experimental charges.`
  - `Only a brief observation was feasible, however, given the number in the sample.`
  - `While the anode layer was slightly thicker than 13 μm, this was a minor deficit.`
  - `Solutions using (q=1) differed slightly from the analytical solutions.`
  - `Future work should therefore include numerical diffusion effects in the calculation of permeability.`
  - `A further study will examine dc-dc converters to determine whether these efficiencies can be achieved in practice.`
  - `This type of control saturation is fairly common and therefore of no significance.`
  - `Although centrifugation could not remove all the excess solid drug, the amount remaining was negligible.`
- 写作检查：我是否在结果部分就提了问题（而非拖到讨论才第一次承认）？是否用弱化词控制了它对读者信心的影响？

### 句子位 7：提及结果的潜在含义/应用（+ happy words）
- 作用：收窄之后开始"向外打开"，用 suggest/indicate 类动词点出含义，并回应引言/摘要中的目标词。这为进入讨论部分铺路。
- 句式模板：
  - `These results suggest that ___ may ___.`
  - `Nevertheless, the data obtained here suggest that ___ may provide more accurate ___ than ___.`
  - `This implies/indicates that ___.` / `It seems that ___.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `These results suggest that NO2 levels along bike paths separated from vehicle traffic may not match those of BC at all times throughout the day.`
  - `The temperature of the Ti target appeared to be somewhat critical.`
  - `The identified pattern is potentially indicative of altered cellular processes.`
  - `Therefore, from these results it could be inferred that the sealant did not improve the coating protective performance.`
- 写作检查：我是否用引言/摘要中出现过的词（如 more accurate）来呼应目标？含义的表达力度是否与结果强度匹配（可用 may/suggest/appear to 缓和）？

### 常用短语速查（Unit 3, pp. 169–182）

- 邀请看图：`Figure 1 shows/illustrates/presents…`、`As can be seen from/in Fig. 1, …`、`The data in Fig. 1 reveal that…`、`(see Fig. 1)`。
- 客观描述结果：`was higher/lower/constant/unchanged/identical`、`increased/decreased/peaked/rose/fell/varied/remained`、`was obtained/was observed`。
- 主观评论（一般）：`striking, dramatic, marked, clear, considerable, remarkable, substantial, slight, minor, negligible, broadly, generally, unexpected`。
- 数量评论（五组）：放大 `as many as, considerable, substantial, marked`；缩小 `only, barely, as few as, slight, minor`；强调 `even higher/lower, much higher, significantly, markedly, remarkably`；近似 `approximately, close to, roughly, virtually, slightly more/less`；不表态 `fairly, relatively, quite, somewhat, moderate`。
- 频率评论（十级）：`always / invariably → usually / generally → frequently / often → more often than not → as often as not → sometimes → occasionally → rarely / seldom → hardly ever → never`。
- "happy" 词：`accurate, advantageous, effective, efficient, excellent, reliable, satisfactory, successful, superior, valuable, simple, feasible`。
- 比较：`consistent with, in line with, in good agreement with, comparable to, contrary to, in contrast to, similar, unlike, corroborate, support, contradict, differ from`。
- 问题弱化：`unfortunately, negligible, not significant, less than ideal, of no consequence, unavoidable, not investigated, not within the scope of this study`。
- 含义/解释：`suggest, indicate, imply, infer, deduce, signify`；`it seems/appears that…, likely, possibly, presumably, probably, could be explained by, in part due to, it may be concluded that`。

## 语言与写作技巧要点（Unit 3.5, pp. 183–186：确定性连续体 Certainty Continuum）

从"推测"到"证明"是一个连续体；写作前先定位你对结果的信心程度与承诺程度，再选词。

### 选对因果动词（表达因果关系的强弱）
- 强/直接因果：`cause, produce, be due to, result in, give rise to, originate in`
- 部分原因：`contribute to, be a factor in`
- 因果过程/链条：`lead to, drive, account for`
- 弱/间接关联（不指明因果方向）：`be related to, be linked to, be associated with, be connected to`
- 注意冠词与介词：`a cause of`（还有别的原因）vs `the cause of`（唯一原因）；`X results in Y`（Y 是结果）vs `X results from Y`（X 是结果）。

### 时态表达信心
- `We found that sunbathing was related to cancer.`（过去时：仅本研究发现，不表永久真理）
- `We found that sunbathing is related to cancer.`（现在时：视为可靠的永久真理）

### 降低风险的措辞（risk-reducers）
- `it appears that… / there is evidence to indicate that… / seem(s) to / tend to / probably / possibly / in some cases`，以及情态动词 may/might。
- 例：`The temperature of the Ti target appeared to be somewhat critical.`；`Therefore, from these results it could be inferred that the sealant did not improve the coating protective performance.`（书中原句）
