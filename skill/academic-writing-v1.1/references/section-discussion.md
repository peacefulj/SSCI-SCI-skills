# 讨论（Discussion）逐句写作引导

来源：Glasman-Deal, *Science Research Writing for Native and Non-Native Speakers of English*, 2nd ed.（World Scientific, 2021），Unit 4（pp. 192–208 建立模型；pp. 221–228 语言与句式）。

## 句子级结构（按写作顺序）

讨论部分与引言对称（引言由宽收窄，讨论由窄再放宽），核心功能是"说出结果意味着什么"。下面 7 个"句子功能位"即书中 Generic Discussion Model 的句子级展开；各成分顺序较灵活，但靠上的通常靠前、靠下的通常靠后：

### 句子位 1：陈述研究的成就/贡献（开场）
- 作用：开门见山地说清本研究完成了什么、对领域有何贡献。这为全文设立"take-home message"，并回应只看标题+摘要+讨论的读者。（也可先用一句话宣布讨论结构，但更常见的是直接说成就。）
- 句式模板：
  - `To our knowledge, the study reported here is the largest/first ___ of ___, and provides a key contribution to ___.`
  - `Our study is the first to ___.`
  - `This study extends previous research by ___.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `To our knowledge, the study reported here is the largest study of patients who have survived the viral disease V2D, and provides a key contribution to the understanding of post-V2D health issues following infection.`
  - `Our study is the first to record a wide range of post-V2D complications...`
  - `This results in a cost-effective approach which significantly improves scalability.`
  - `We describe not only neutral but also ionized systems with unprecedented accuracy.`
  - `This work demonstrates that multimodel predictions can provide a more reliable estimate of uncertainty.`
  - `The system described here enables fast and easy analysis of LRRC8 proteins.`
  - `The rapid, easy-to-perform mRNA-based method presented here is a robust approach to detect rare coding.`
  - `The model is thus both realistic and flexible, taking advantage of both types of computational phantoms.`
  - `The analytical method described here removes the need for difficult and time consuming pre-treatment.`
- 写作检查：成就（内部、回应引言问题）与贡献（对外、影响知识或应用）我是否都表达清楚了？是否用与引言相同的动词（如引言说 identify，讨论也用 identify）形成呼应、避免换同义词造成歧义？

### 句子位 2：重访背景/文献/空白/目标以"重启"读者
- 作用：很多读者只读标题+摘要+讨论，需在这里重述相关背景、空白、目标或方法要点，让讨论能独立成立；重访时用与原文相同的词形成"回声"。
- 句式模板：
  - `___ is a multifactorial ___, frequently associated with ___.`（重述背景）
  - `Since ___, the most common models result from ___.`（重述空白/目标）
  - `In this study, ___ was used as a reliable model of ___.`（重述方法）
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `IBS is a multifactorial disease, frequently associated with psychological distress and characterized by altered gastrointestinal motor function...`
  - `In this study, WAS was used as a reliable model of chronic psychological stress.`
  - `Since stressful life events contribute to the IBS symptom exacerbation, the most common animal models reproducing "IBS-like" symptoms result from the use of central nervous system-directed stressors.`
- 写作检查：我重访的部分是否与引言用词一致（形成"回声"）？读者不读前文也能看懂讨论吗？是否只重访了"为叙述服务"的部分，而非整段复述？

### 句子位 3：重访关键结果并探讨其含义
- 作用：简要重述关键结果作为证据锚点，然后重点展开"这意味着什么"（而非再次罗列结果）。结果部分负责"是什么"，讨论部分负责"意味着什么"。
- 句式模板：
  - `A key finding was that ___. This has clear implications for ___.`
  - `This was particularly true in relation to ___.`
  - `These results indicate/suggest that ___.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `A key finding was that some post-V2D health issues appear to be age-related... This has clear implications for post-V2D-related public health management...`
  - `It is conceivable that such a mechanism could play a role in many other disease states.`
  - `A tentative explanation is that the colloidal system may be agglomerated at that treatment level.`
  - `We hypothesize that our work could serve as a catalyst for new forms of real-time interventions.`
- 时态提示：动词时态传达信心——过去时（appeared to be）=仅本研究发现；现在时（appear to be）=视为永久真理。选择要与结果强度匹配。
- 写作检查：我是在"重述结果"后继续往前走（解释含义），还是只是换个说法重复结果？时态是否与我对结果的信心匹配？

### 句子位 4：映射到文献/知识（比较、支持或挑战）
- 作用：把本研究放上已有知识地图——结果与他人一致（支持/验证）、不一致（挑战/质疑），从而定位贡献。每个引文的功能要通过句首短语显式表达。
- 句式模板：
  - `We first demonstrated that ___, consistent with the literature (refs).`
  - `In contrast, [ref] reported ___.`
  - `In line with this, a recent study by [ref] clearly showed that ___.`
  - `Likewise, the role of ___ has been proposed by [ref].`
  - `On the basis of these results, we challenge the assumptions made by ___.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `We first demonstrated that a 4-day WAS induced intestinal epithelial barrier impairment, consistent with the literature (2, 4, 45).`
  - `In contrast, O'Malley et al. (34) reported an increased colonic mucus secretion...`
  - `Rather than being excluded as is often suggested, the sulfate reducers seemed to be thriving.`
  - `The presented structure-based approach complements existing experimental methods.`
  - `This study extends previous research by analysing corporate brand and industry image simultaneously.`
  - `Our data therefore provide support for the theory proposed by Stephen Robbins.`
  - `These results are consistent with the theory that fungal infection leads to plant death.`
  - `The frequency domain algorithm proposed here resembles the so-called Frisch Scheme approach.`
  - `Unlike existing control schemes, in this scheme the parameters can be freely changed during operation.`
  - `This is in agreement with results from recent studies which suggest that iron reducers can modify their rate of respiration.`
  - `To verify this assumption, AFM was used, as previously depicted for the characterization of conformational and hydrodynamic properties of individual mucin polymers (7, 38).`
  - `In line with this, a recent in vivo study by Wrzosek et al. (47) clearly showed that commensal bacteria... could directly influence goblet cell development...`
  - `On the basis of these results, we challenge the assumptions made by existing physical-layer security systems.`
  - `The current study therefore both confirmed and expanded prior research into these mechanisms.`
- 写作检查：每个引文的功能（支持？对比？铺垫？）是否通过句首短语（In contrast / In line with / Likewise / To verify this assumption）显式表达，而非让读者自己猜？引用数量是否大致匹配目标期刊的惯例？

### 句子位 5：指出局限并提出未来工作建议
- 作用：诚实指出研究的不足或未解答的问题，并顺势给出明确的未来研究方向（邀请同行接续你的工作）。
- 句式模板：
  - `Some questions remain; for example, ___. However, this was not addressed formally during the study.`
  - `A direct comparison with ___ would provide valuable data to support ___.`
  - `Future work should ___.`（邀请他人）/ `Future studies should investigate whether ___.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `Some questions remain; for example, anecdotal evidence... suggested that there was a positive correlation between viral load... and the severity of post-V2D health issues. However, this was not addressed formally during the study.`
  - `Future studies should investigate whether reducing household air pollution may lead to improvement in cardiac morbidity.`
  - `Future work should focus on a qualitative analysis of patient-reported benefits of group therapy.`
  - `Further work is in progress to determine whether a different drought response could help these trees to survive.`
  - `Developing a model able to answer all these questions is an exciting challenge for future studies.`
  - `Recommendations for future studies include an investigation of polysaccharide redundancy during cell wall assembly.`
  - `In the future, it would be of interest to develop a more efficient numerical method for simulating these system dynamics.`
  - `To support these findings, variations in cfDNA test performance should be investigated further.`
- 写作检查：未来工作是否具体（可执行）而非空泛？是否用 should（邀请他人）/ will（我自己的计划）区分清楚？局限是否与结果/方法部分已提过的问题保持一致？

### 句子位 6：重申成就/贡献/影响
- 作用：在讨论收尾处再次强调研究的价值（成就/贡献是讨论中"最自由流动"的成分，可开头结尾都出现）。
- 句式模板：
  - `The results of our study provide compelling evidence that ___.`
  - `The study provides a first step towards ___.`
  - `We achieved outstanding performance compared to ___.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `...the results of our study provide compelling evidence that follow-up of all convalescent patients is warranted for at least 10 months after discharge from hospital.`
  - `The study provides a first step towards a better representation of southern African dust sources in dust emission models.`
  - `We achieved outstanding performance compared to similar catalysts reported in the literature.`
  - `This work demonstrates that multimodel predictions can provide a more reliable estimate of uncertainty.`
  - `The system described here provides novel means to systematically study this in diseased tissues.`
  - `A straightforward analysis procedure is presented which enables accurate prediction.`
- 写作检查：我是否明确、不低调地写出了研究的价值（文化上"谦虚"可能导致价值被写得不可识别）？是否过度承诺、超出数据支撑？

### 句子位 7：指出潜在应用
- 作用：面向"真实世界"说明研究可用于何处，收尾全文。
- 句式模板：
  - `In addition, the study provides ___ guidance for ___ that includes ___.`
  - `The approach presented here is suitable/applicable for ___.`
  - `The proposed technique could be implemented in ___.`
  - `This work shows that ___ is/are feasible for widespread use.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `In addition, the study provides evidence-based guidance for pragmatic public health recommendations that include age-related follow-up and treatment of post-V2D complications.`
  - `This work shows that low-cost air quality sensor networks are feasible for widespread use.`
  - `The approach presented here is also suitable for problems where structural information is incomplete.`
  - `The data reported in this study could eventually lead to recommendations to guide optimal Clozapine use.`
  - `The proposed technique could be implemented widely within the bioprocess industry.`
  - `The dissection protocol outlined here is appropriate for all long bone analyses including ex vivo imaging.`
  - `The experimental paradigm outlined here could be used to evaluate future robotic social agents.`
  - `Although it focuses on the specific case of supernovae, the concepts presented here are applicable to other astrophysical cases.`
  - `We see miR-CLIP as a broadly applicable approach that can be used in conjunction with others.`
  - `Our protocol will enable the transport and storage of samples, thereby facilitating mitochondrial function analysis of liver biopsies.`
- 写作检查：即使研究暂无直接应用，我是否查了引言与目标文献中"这类研究可用于何处"的说法，避免漏掉可写的应用？

### 常用短语速查（Unit 4, pp. 221–228）

- 映射到文献：`consistent with, in agreement with, in line with, comparable to, analogous to, contrary to, in contrast to, unlike, similar, identical to, new/novel, the first of its kind, to the best of our knowledge`；动词 `confirm, corroborate, support, substantiate, validate, contradict, refute, challenge, extend, expand, complement, mirror, resemble, differ from, pave the way for, shed new light on, provide insight into`。
- 提炼/探讨含义：`plausible, potential, tentative, to hypothesise, to postulate, to speculate, to theorise`；`it is conceivable that…, it is reasonable to assume that…, we cannot rule out…, this points to…`。
- 成就/贡献："happy" 词 `accurate, effective, efficient, robust, significant, novel, systematic, valid, convincing, practical, cost-effective, low-cost`；"very happy"（!-substitutes）词 `compelling, crucial, dramatic, exceptional, extraordinary, outstanding, remarkable, striking, surprising, unprecedented, unique, vital`。
- 当前与未来工作：`future work/studies should…, further work is needed, promising, holds promise, a/the starting point, we recommend, we suggest, would be beneficial/useful, worth investigating, further work is in progress`。
- 应用：`to apply, to enable, to facilitate, to generalise, to implement, to lead to, to serve as, to utilise`；`applicable, appropriate, feasible, practicable, practical, realistic, suitable, viable, eventually, in due course, in future`。

## 语言与写作技巧要点（Unit 4.5, pp. 229–241：情态动词）

讨论部分大量使用情态动词表达确定性/承诺程度，含义对照（书中 Key 归纳）：
- 能力 can/could：`The model can predict a wide range of experimental data.`（= is able to）
- 可能性 may/might/could：`These interactions may/might/could be the same for each species.`（= It is possible that...）
- 预期/很可能 should：`The ratio should remain constant if the expansion is uniform.`（= is expected to / is likely to / will probably）
- 显然/推断 must：`This must be the result of direct collision...`（= It is obvious that...）
- 建议 should：`The tubes should be centrifuged before the experiment.`（= It is advisable to...）
- 必要 must/need to/have to：`The tubes must/need to/have to be centrifuged...`（= It is necessary to...）
- 否定注意：`must not`（禁止/不允许）≠ `does not have to`（非必须）。
- 过去式注意：`should have + 过去分词` 常指"本该发生而未发生"；`should not have` 常指"本不该发生而发生了"。
- 常见搭配：`it is conceivable that… could…`、`may ultimately lead to…`、`could, in part, signal…`。
- 若不确定用 can 还是 be able to，用 be able to；未来时用 `will be able to`；could 兼有"可能"与"能够"两义，需按语境消歧。
