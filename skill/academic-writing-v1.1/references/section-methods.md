# 方法（Methods）逐句写作引导

来源：Glasman-Deal, *Science Research Writing for Native and Non-Native Speakers of English*, 2nd ed.（World Scientific, 2021），Unit 2（pp. 77–88 建立模型；pp. 99–112 语言与句式）。

## 句子级结构（按写作顺序）

方法部分不只是"记录你做了什么"，还要让读者了解并接受你的选择（`I had good reasons for those decisions`）。下面 7 个"句子功能位"即书中 Generic Methods Model 的句子级展开；它是"菜单式"的——按需要选用，未必全部用上：

### 句子位 1：概述方法 / 重述目标 / 说明材料来源
- 作用：用一句"总—分"的概述让读者先看到全貌，再进入细节（show the wall before the bricks）。三种常见开头：①概述工作参数（数量、材料、目的）；②说明材料/设备来源或性质；③回指上一节的目标/要解决的问题。
- 句式模板：
  - `The current investigation involved ___ to determine ___.`
  - `The aim of this study was to ___.`（重述目标）
  - `___ was purchased from ___.` / `___ was provided by ___.` / `___ was a kind gift from ___.`（说明来源）
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `The current investigation involved collecting and analysing groundwater samples from six wells in the Mahomet Aquifer region to determine levels of dissolved CO2.`
  - `A three-step approach was used to determine the muscle force.`
  - `The fetal bovine serum used in this study was provided by Gibco, UK.`
  - `Dextran standard 5000 was purchased from Sigma Aldrich.`
  - `The cell lines were a kind gift from Dr David Louis (Massachusetts General Hospital, MA, USA).`
  - `A laboratory-scale fixed-bed reactor was used in this study.`
  - `The inclusion criteria used here are a modified version of those in a previous study.`
  - `All volumetric lung images were acquired from healthy asthmatic subjects.`
  - `The extracts were obtained by using two different methods.`
- 写作检查：读者能否从这一句同时知道"做了什么 + 为什么做（目标）+ 材料/样本来源"？我的开头是否"总—分"（先框架后细节），而非一上来就堆具体细节？

### 句子位 2：提供背景信息并论证选择
- 作用：给出读者理解具体方法所需的背景（如材料性质、地点），并为你的选择提供理由——让读者理解并接受"为什么这么做"。
- 句式模板：
  - `___ is in ___, and since ___, ___ has/have been frequently used to ___ [refs].`
  - `___ was chosen because ___.` / `___ was selected on the basis of ___.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `This region is in the Midwestern United States, and since groundwater in this area contains virtually no modern contaminants, wells in this area have been frequently used to interpret groundwater evolution.`
  - `For brevity, these equations are not included in this paper.`
  - `To make the problem tractable, we reduced the number of possible scenarios.`
  - `The advantage of using three-dimensional analysis was that the out-of-plane stress field could be obtained.`
  - `For simplicity, only a single value was analysed.`
  - `The survey was administered during the spring, which avoided problems of low activity during the summer months.`
  - `Our control drug was given to all patients, therefore we were able to separate the effects of increased pH from other factors.`
  - `For verification purposes, tests were repeated for 9 different combinations of temperature and strain rate.`
  - `Varying the diameter required the machine to be recalibrated, which was rather time-consuming.`
- 写作检查：我的选择理由对我是"显然"的，但读者是否需要我明说？我是否用 in order to / because / since 把"为什么这么做"写出来了（如 `An Ag wire was attached... in order to form an electrical contact`）？

### 句子位 3：对所用程序作一般性陈述（段落引导句）
- 作用：用一句概括性的话开启一个方法段落，为后面的细节建立框架；常见开头如 `Most of the tests`、`In all cases`。
- 句式模板：
  - `A total of ___ samples were collected for analysis; ___ from each ___.`
  - `In all cases, ___.` / `Most of the tests ___.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `A total of 18 3 mL samples were collected for analysis; three from each well.`
  - `In the majority of the tests, the chickens were kept in a temperature test chamber.`
- 写作检查：这一段有没有一句"段落引导句"让读者先知道这个段落在讲什么？

### 句子位 4：详述材料与方法步骤（顺序 + 谨慎 + 理由）
- 作用：给出可复现的具体细节——用了什么、按什么顺序、做得多么仔细、每一步为什么。顺序用精确的"序列语言"，而非只写 then/next。
- 句式模板：
  - `Prior to ___, ___ was/were ___ using ___. ... until ___, at which point ___.`
  - 谨慎语言：`carefully / tightly / thoroughly / strictly / independently ___`
  - 理由：`to prevent ___` / `in order to form ___` / `which ensured ___`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `Prior to collection, stagnant water was pumped out of the wells using a Grundfos® Redi-Flo II electric submersible pump. pH levels were monitored... until stable pH readings were obtained, at which point the samples were taken.`
  - `The samples were transferred to amber glass bottles, which were sealed tightly and then stored under refrigeration at 5°C to prevent contamination...`
  - `Care was taken to maintain strict anaerobic conditions during extract preparation.`
  - `These cells were then washed thoroughly at least three times.`
  - `The specimen was monitored constantly for 24 hours.`
  - `The specimen was tightly clamped at two corners to minimize any unexpected gaps.`
  - `To prevent an emulsion from forming, only gentle, repeated inversion was used.`
  - `Frequent readings were taken to update the stress conditions smoothly.`
  - `We used a reliable analytical method based on high-performance liquid chromatography.`
  - `6 pristine specimens were handpicked under a microscope.`
- 写作检查：步骤顺序是否用了精确顺序词（prior to / until / at which point / subsequently / simultaneously）而非只有 then？我是否既写出"做了什么"，也写出"做得多仔细"（副词）和"为什么"（in order to）？

### 句子位 5：描述图/表示意图
- 作用：方法部分常含图、表、照片或示意装置图，需用空间关系语言描述其结构/布局，让读者"看到"装置或流程。
- 句式模板：
  - `The apparatus consists of a ___ fitted with ___.`
  - `___ was mounted on top of ___, while ___ was placed underneath/above/below ___.`
  - `___ was/were positioned on opposite sides.` / `___ is aligned with ___.`
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `The apparatus consists of a circular tube fitted with rectangular-winglet tape (RWT) vortex generators.`
  - `A scanner was mounted on top of a metal structure, while pots were placed underneath, over a cart moving at constant speed...`
  - `Measuring stations were spaced 20 cm apart, starting 2 cm downstream of the pipe entrance.`
  - `The source light is polarised horizontally and the sample beam can be scanned laterally.`
  - `The microtubules that were embedded in collagen gel were oriented perpendicular to the direction of the magnetic field.`
  - `In the experiment, source and detector were positioned on opposite sides.`
- 写作检查：读者能否凭文字在脑中重构装置/流程的空间布局？方位词（above/underneath/adjacent/perpendicular to）是否精确、无歧义？

### 句子位 6：引用 / 比较他人的材料或方法（三选一）
- 作用：把你的方法映射到已有方法——相同、相似、还是显著不同（不同点可能就是你的贡献）。这是贯穿全文"文献线索"在方法部分的落点。
- 句式模板：
  - 相同：`___ was carried out as described by/in [ref].`（`as in [ref]` / `using the method of [ref]` / `identical to that previously reported in [ref]`）
  - 相似：`___ was adapted from [ref].` / `___ was a modified version of ___.`（`based on` / `essentially the same as` / `with some modifications`）
  - 显著不同：`Unlike [ref], we ___.` / `Instead of ___, we placed ___.`（`however, in this study, ___` / `with the following modifications:`）
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `The analytical method was adapted from British Pharmacopeia.`
  - `The method used was identical to that previously reported in ref. 17.`
  - `The method was essentially the same as that previously described elsewhere.`
  - `We modified the Du and Parker filter to address these shortcomings and we refer to this modified filter as the MaxCurve filter.`
  - `However, in this study, instead of using a coil around the membrane cell, we placed the coil on top of the cell.`
  - `We follow Sobral et al. (2013) and apply one magnitude of dust extinction to all of our HAEs.`
  - `Data were collected using a revised version of the Portrait Values Questionnaire.`
  - `Activated calf thymus DNA was prepared as described by Pedrali-Noy and Weissbach (17).`
- 写作检查：我的方法与所引文献是"相同/相似/显著不同"哪一种？若显著不同，我是否明确写出了差别（尤其当差别带来改进时）？引用位置是否准确（by+人名 / in+文献）？

### 句子位 7：指出方法中的问题/局限
- 作用：主动、专业地承认方法中的困难——弱化其影响、弱化你的责任、放大好的方面、或给出解决/未来方向。第一次出现的位置就提（而非留到文末），反而增强可信度。
- 句式模板：
  - `Although ___, the amount remaining was negligible.`（弱化：`slightly/negligible/minor/brief/not significant`）
  - `It was slightly difficult to ___, probably owing to ___.`
  - 给出方向：`Future work should ___.`（邀请他人）/ `Future work will ___.`（我自己的计划）
- 例句（书中原句）：
  - `Although centrifugation did not remove all the excess solid drug, the amount remaining was negligible.`
  - `It was slightly difficult to deposit a uniform film of the liquid, probably owing to its low viscosity.`
  - `Despite our routine of monthly examinations, follow-up was not perfect.`
  - `Although some duplication was unavoidable, this was minimized by the frequent use of cross references.`
  - `The probability is very small, and hence we believe such contaminants have only a negligible effect on this study.`
  - `Inevitably, considerable computation was involved.`
  - `This procedure was slightly problematic in that the program reported successful setup even if a nonexistent file was specified.`
  - `Due to the lack of multiwavelength data it was not possible to apply additional broad-band selections.`
- 写作检查：我是否在"第一次出现问题的位置"就提了它？语言是否既诚实又不显得失职（弱化影响、给出原因或解决）？

### 常用短语速查（Unit 2, pp. 99–112）

- 材料/样本来源：`was purchased (from) / was provided (by) / was obtained / was a kind gift (from) / was used as received`。
- 空间关系（描述设备/图示）：`above/below, adjacent, alongside, on top of, perpendicular to, parallel to, downstream/upstream, mounted on, positioned on`；动词 `align, arrange, assemble, attach, connect, embed, encase, enclose, fasten, fit, fix, join, locate, mount, orient, place, position, situate, surround`。
- 精确操作动词（被动）：`was adapted, was applied, was calculated, was calibrated, was measured, was prepared, was recorded, was simulated, was quantified, was optimised`。
- 序列语言（八组，精确表达时间与先后）：
  - 开始前：`beforehand, previously, prior to, initially, formerly, in advance`
  - 开始/第一步：`at first, at the beginning, firstly, to begin with, to start with`
  - 顺序/步骤：`after, afterwards, followed by, following, next, subsequently, then, prior to`
  - 短间隔之后：`soon, shortly after, quickly, immediately`
  - 较晚/长间隔之后：`later, later on, eventually, in due course, towards the end`
  - 几乎/恰好同时：`as, as soon as, at the same time, at that point, meanwhile, simultaneously, until, when, while`
  - 结束/最后一步：`finally, at the end, in the end, lastly`
  - 结束之后：`afterwards, later, later on, subsequently, eventually`
- 论证选择（原因/目的）：`in order to, so as to, to this end, chosen for, for simplicity, for convenience, which allowed, by doing…we were able to, our aim was to`。
- 表明"谨慎"（副词）：`carefully, thoroughly, strictly, accurately, precisely, tightly, independently, repeatedly, randomly, at least`。
- 问题弱化：`negligible, minimal, minor, slight(ly), immaterial, not significant, less than ideal, unavoidable, time-consuming`。

## 语言与写作技巧要点（Unit 2.5, pp. 113–126）

### 无施动者被动（agentless passive）与动词时态
- 方法部分常用"无施动者被动"（`was found` 而非 `was found by us`），因为"谁做的"通常不言自明，可避免重复与歧义。
- 时态区分意义，用错会改变含义：
  - 报告你做了什么 → 过去时无施动者被动：`X was collected/measured/recorded.`
  - 描述标准程序/设备 → 现在时无施动者被动：`X is collected/measured/recorded.`（并常需引文）
- 例：`Samples were shipped directly to two separate laboratories...`（过去时，报告本文做法）；`This method uses a carbon analyser with a detection limit of 0.050 mg L−1...`（现在时，描述该方法的固有特性）。

### 时态与"归属性"（ownership）
- 无施动者被动在同一句里可能既像"你做的"又像"标准做法"，因此要靠时态和引文让读者分清：你的工作（过去时）、已有的标准方法（现在时 + 引文）、以及需要注明出处的他人方法（引文）。
